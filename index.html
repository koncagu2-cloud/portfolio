ALIGN / Independent AI-assisted concept

A match is
a claim.
Show the evidence.

A job-discovery prototype that separates listing facts, candidate preferences and unknowns.

Independent AI-assisted concept · Product direction and iterative feedback.

Fictional listings and candidate data. No real application delivery or employer participation.

Try the prototype ↗Read the case study ↗
Design decision and contribution

A responsive job-discovery prototype separates listing facts, candidate preferences and unknowns before application review. The premise comes from the brief, not participant research.

My role / Independent AI-assisted conceptProduct direction and iterative feedback; AI-assisted design and implementation. Decision: keep listing facts, candidate preferences and unknowns distinct.

01 / Conditions

Facts stay facts.

Listing conditions stay separate from the candidate’s preferences.

02 / Evidence

Claims have sources.

A self-declared qualification is not verified evidence.

03 / Unknowns

Missing stays visible.

Missing seniority is shown as not specified, rather than inferred from a title.

Explore the original comparison and responsive discovery artifacts

My contributionProduct direction and iterative feedback. AI-assisted design exploration, documentation, implementation and checks. No market validation or independent participant results are claimed.
